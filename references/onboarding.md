# Onboarding

Use on first use or when the candidate profile needs revision. Keep intake in the private local workspace.

## First-use flow

1. Detect whether an existing profile is available. Review its source evidence, unresolved fields and confirmation state; reuse confirmed facts only after checking for changes. Otherwise begin a new profile.
2. Confirm the intended stream: Professional, Casual/Part-time, or both. Ask about target roles, location, work arrangement and employment preferences; do not silently choose a stream.
3. Read and review a supplied resume, then interview for missing context. With no resume, interview about background, responsibilities, tools, dates and supported outcomes, then create a Resume Draft. In either route, show the draft or extracted facts for correction; a polished sentence is not new evidence.
4. Present a Candidate Profile Summary with evidence sources and unresolved questions. Include onshore/offshore location, work rights and restrictions, sponsorship needs, role intent, relevant experience, qualifications, availability, commute, salary or hourly-rate preferences, and certificates where relevant. Ask only for facts needed for the intended work.
   A stated city of residence or target location does not establish the candidate's onshore/offshore location mode. Never infer or assume location mode from a city, address or location preference; ask the candidate to confirm onshore or offshore explicitly and leave it unknown until they do.
5. Ask the candidate to confirm the profile before constructing search filters. Do not treat silence, a prior resume upload, or approval of wording as confirmation of missing facts.
6. Build candidate-controlled search filters using [references/search-filters.md](search-filters.md). Show the proposed settings and Top N, then confirm the filters before Discovery begins.
7. Start Discovery only with both confirmations in place. If profile facts or filters materially change, show the change and renew the affected confirmation before using it.

At each onboarding transition, update the private current-state profile with its actual confirmation state and append an intake or confirmation event to the append-only event log. Record pending as pending rather than inventing confirmation; a conversational summary does not replace the current-state update or history event.

## Uncertainty and handoff

Unknown or missing facts remain unknown; never assume, infer or guess them. Ask a focused question or record what evidence is needed. A resumed session must check the latest confirmed profile and filter rather than repeat intake automatically.

Keep sensitive sharing and optional schedules separate under [references/privacy-and-consent.md](privacy-and-consent.md).
