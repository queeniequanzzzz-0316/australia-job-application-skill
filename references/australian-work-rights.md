# Australian work rights

Operate in Australia only: candidate location may be onshore or offshore, but target jobs must be in the Australian market. Confirm and record onshore versus offshore status; do not infer it from nationality, contact details or a resume address.

## Verification procedure

1. Keep work rights unknown until confirmed by the candidate or supported by evidence. Distinguish a candidate statement from independent verification using [references/job-screening-and-scoring.md](job-screening-and-scoring.md). Record restrictions, expiry and sponsorship need only when supported; resolve conflicts before treating eligibility as established.
2. Consult the Department of Home Affairs at runtime to verify current visa and work-rights rules relevant to the candidate. Record the official source, retrieval date, applicable conditions and unresolved limits of the check.
3. Use current VEVO information to verify visa conditions where applicable and authorised. Ask for candidate-controlled evidence or confirmation when access is unavailable; never claim a VEVO check occurred when it did not. Keep sensitive identifiers private under [references/privacy-and-consent.md](privacy-and-consent.md).
4. Compare the evidenced rights and location with the actual job requirements, including sponsorship and work restrictions. Missing evidence is an unresolved gap; confirmed incompatibility is a hard blocker. A job-board sponsorship signal alone does not establish employer sponsorship or candidate eligibility.

## Boundary

Provide factual verification of source information, not immigration advice or a personalised migration strategy. If interpretation is unresolved, identify the question and direct the candidate to the relevant official source or a qualified adviser.

Never hardcode visa policy numbers or rely on frozen rules. Recheck current official information when making a material eligibility decision. Never guarantee a visa, sponsorship, permanent residence or PR outcome, or present job selection as a guaranteed migration pathway.
