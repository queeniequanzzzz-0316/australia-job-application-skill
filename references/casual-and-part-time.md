# Casual and part-time screening

Apply the verification, hard gates, verdicts and Evidence Audit in [references/job-screening-and-scoring.md](job-screening-and-scoring.md). Use these stream-specific defaults instead of Professional weights.

| Factor | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Availability match | 25% |
| Work-rights compatibility | 20% |
| Commute practicality | 15% |
| Relevant experience | 15% |
| Pay and conditions | 10% |
| Required certificates | 10% |
| Employer legitimacy | 5% |

The weights sum to 100. Candidate-defined weights override defaults; confirm replacements and retain a total of 100. Hard blockers cannot be offset by a high score.

## Check before ranking

- Availability: compare confirmed start date, shift times and hours with the actual role. Ask about missing constraints.
- Work rights: verify applicable restrictions using [references/australian-work-rights.md](australian-work-rights.md); do not infer compatibility.
- Commute: compare workplace, shift timing and confirmed travel limits.
- Relevant experience: cite supported responsibilities and skills; retain missing requirements as gaps.
- Pay and conditions: check the stated basis, employment arrangement and relevant award or classification. Consult current Fair Work Ombudsman information at runtime to verify applicable rates and conditions; record the source and date. If coverage or classification is uncertain, surface the uncertainty rather than claim compliance.
- Required certificates: check whether the role requires particular certificates or licences, and whether candidate evidence supports current validity.
- Employer legitimacy: verify the employer and application entry; flag payment requests, inconsistent identities or unverifiable claims before shortlisting.

Never hardcode a wage amount or rely on frozen rates or conditions. Compare the actual offer with current authoritative information; do not give legal advice or guarantee compliance. Return the same evidence-grounded shortlist output as the Professional stream.
